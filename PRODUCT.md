# PRODUCT.md - Sentinel

## Register

**product** (with one brand surface). The dashboard, park pages, dossier and backtest are tools:
design serves the data. `/` is the only brand surface, and exists to explain the tool to someone
who has never seen it.

## What it is

An AI operations centre for protected areas. Satellite fire detections are triaged against park
boundaries with pure geometry; the parks that surface are then investigated with multimodal AI
(camera images, acoustic recordings); findings are fused into a ranger briefing, spoken aloud by
a live voice agent, and compiled into an evidence packet.

## Who uses it

1. **A ranger in a vehicle at night.** Cannot look at a screen. Voice first, hands free.
2. **A park manager at a desk.** Wants the map, the feed, the briefing, and to ask questions.
3. **An investigator building a case.** Wants a printable packet and, above all, an explicit
   statement of what the evidence does NOT prove.
4. **A first-time visitor / hackathon judge.** No conservation or GIS background. Must understand
   every panel without a glossary. This audience drives the copy standard.

## Purpose

Detection that ends in a dashboard changes nothing. The product exists to close the loop between
a satellite pixel and a person who can act.

## Brand personality

Calm, exact, faintly grave. A duty officer on a night watch, not a growth product. Never chirpy,
never salesy. The subject matter is fires and poaching; the interface should feel like it takes
that seriously without theatrics.

## Anti-references

- Security-vendor fear marketing (red klaxons, threat counters spinning up).
- "AI copilot" purple gradients and glow.
- Consumer eco-brand warmth (cream, sage, hand-drawn leaves). This is an operations tool.
- Dashboards that imply certainty the data does not support.

## Strategic design principles

1. **Honesty is a design constraint, not a disclaimer.** Every surface that shows a machine
   classification must show its confidence and say "reported", never "confirmed". Replayed feeds
   are labelled wherever they appear.
2. **Plain language over domain jargon.** FRP, VIIRS, severity integers and "field simulation" are
   meaningless to a first-time reader. Show the plain meaning first, the technical term second.
3. **Never bury a level-1 signal.** Volume of routine data must never outrank a gunshot.
4. **Cost is part of the design.** The AI quota is finite; the interface should make it obvious
   which actions spend it.
